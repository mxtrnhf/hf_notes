# Under / over the enemy ship: how real is the "dive under" advantage? (38 sessions, 2026-10-18)

> **Dated measurement report** (2026-10-18): 38 sessions of `hf_ally` telemetry (builds v0.12.x). It describes the stock AI as it behaved then, not current behaviour; `hf_ai` has changed it since. The design page [ai-hf-ai-design.md](../findings/ai/ai-hf-ai-design.md) cites it as evidence.

Status: **measured from telemetry; the dive-denial tactic of `hf_ai` (v0.4.0 and later, [../mods/hf-ai.md](../mods/hf-ai.md)) was built from it.** Full text with per-cell numbers: `tuning_telemetry/underside_report.md`. Marks: S supported, G guess, FS too few samples. "Under" = player within +-45 deg of straight below the enemy (y points down); hit shell = player-owned logic-100 body ending within 28 u of an enemy (95% have a health drop within 0.16 s). The parts CSV mixes frames: legs, engine_04, cannons are logged in WORLD coordinates, frame parts (hull, armour, fuel, engine_05) in LOCAL; converted with local = rot(world - root, -ang).

## Findings
* **Layout:** armour plates sit on top (mean local y -5), guns/ordnance upper-middle, hull and legs about +1, engines +3.6, fuel lowest (+6.5); Courageous and Navarin carry bombs at the very bottom (+19.6). The lower third holds 26% (light), 28% (medium), 31% (heavy) of part hp: mostly hull, legs, engines.
* **Damage in the lower third** (player-shell-linked, airborne): light 22% (hp share 26%, no underside weakness, FS and against the user's impression), medium 48% (hp share 28%, from under 61%), heavy 42% (from under 54%). Shells from below land in the lower third 53%, from the side 40%, from above 28% (about the hp share): the extra underside damage is mostly geometry (the keel is what a shot from below meets first), not a weaker underside. S for medium/heavy.
* **Hit rate:** hits per shot are ~1.35x higher when the player is under OR above than at the side (S). Damage per hit from under 1.05-1.2x (no large per-hit weakness); from above 0.75-0.88x (armour on top, G). Damage per engaged second 1.6x under and 1.9-2.3x above (G; partly the pilot's own bursts).
* **Under and above are both better for the attacker than the side; above is at least as good as under.** So the defence should not just climb above the attacker.
* **Time under:** the player is under an enemy 39% of enemy-time within 600 u, side 40%, above 21% (random would be 25/50/25); Lightning 43% under, Intrepid 42%, Navarin 16-21% (the Navarin pilot sat above 43%). Dives (n=125): dwell median 3.3 s (p90 9.9 s), entry range 467 u, closest ~250 u; median damage per dive 165 hp under vs 67 above (G). The v0.3.0 tactic did not change this (41% under).
* **What enemies do:** speed unchanged by the player's position (60-73 u/s); with the player above they move down (86%) and back off (-38 u/s), with the player under they circle horizontally (stock waypoint points straight away from the player 8% of the time under vs 43% above) and keep the line of sight. hf_ai overrides at <300 u with the player under: 93% of samples, 85% circling at 60-120 deg: horizontal circling changes the line-of-sight angle slowly.
* **Gun blind spot:** no general one (S): 5 of 7 designs fire more at a player below (median under/side ratio 1.29); Gryphon and Sarma fire less (FS); top-mounted Slogger/Nimrod fire little everywhere (FS). Some guns have all-zero `m_sectors` in the designs (Gladiator x2, Gryphon x3 = blocked by their own design), untested.
* **Kills:** removal rule invisible; removed enemies look like survivors at session end (hp 83% vs 82%); first damage to removal 7.5 s (light), 9 s (medium), 21 s (heavy). "Fastest kills come from dives" cannot be answered from this data.
* **Evasion check:** it is not missing. 4 START for 4 of 4 player missile launches in the two new sessions (two had 2 IGNORED rolls first), 30 STEER and 16 BOOST lines, flare-equipped targets dropped flares 4/4 (8/8 earlier). START comes 270-400 u from the ship, ~1 s before the pass: if it feels absent, the late onset is the likely reason (n=7, FS).

## Proposal: "deny the dive" (text only; priority panic evasion > deny-the-dive > band keeping)
* Goal: keep the line of sight within ~45 deg of horizontal, whichever side the player is on; do NOT simply climb above (above is as good for the attacker).
* Trigger from live values: player-to-ship vector within 45 deg of vertical (|dx| < |dy|), range < 600 u, held >= 1 s (median dive 3.3 s), not in panic, not landed.
* Response: waypoint that increases |dx| fastest (away from the player's x or opposite his lateral velocity), ~|dy| of lateral shift with boost; present the flank, not the keel; descend toward level only with altitude margin, never below the player.
* Risks: terrain at y +160..+420 (estimated), 9.5% of airborne enemy time is at y > 0, arena ~x +-1500, ceiling ~-2000; hard lateral moves; a wide-moving enemy may expose its wingman.
* Metrics (v0.3.0 sessions are the control): share of near-range engaged time spent under/over (baseline ~60%), median under-episode dwell (3.0-3.3 s), player hit fraction (0.65 under/above vs 0.48 side at <300 u), damage per engaged second, first damage to removal.
