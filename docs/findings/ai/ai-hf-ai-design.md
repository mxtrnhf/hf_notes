# AI tactics for Highfleet: `hf_ai` design and the existing `ai_flares` algorithm

Status (2026-10-18, evening): `hf_ai` v0.1.0 (log-only steering hook) ran in game and works (626 waypoint rows, all exactly 200 u). v0.2.0 (commit 2c563f5, local) ports qol `ai_flares` (config `Modloader/config/hf_ai.json`, `ai_flares.enabled` default false, optional `log_panic` trace); built and unit-tested (24 tests), **never run in game, not installed**. Pipeline stages in the steering hook: stock waypoint, `decide(ctx)` tactics, panic override, CSV row, store. Tags: legend in `../../README.md`. Data for tuning: [ai-telemetry.md](ai-telemetry.md); background and stages: "Where to take the AI" in [battle-ally.md](battle-ally.md).

## Decisions
* **A Modloader DLL is the only way to change how the AI flies** [code]: steering lives in the per-ship tick `FUN_140062f90` and aiming in `FUN_14007a8d0`, compiled code with no script or data hook. Data mods (`.seria`) change what the AI flies (designs, loadouts, fleet makeup, weapon ranges), not steering, timing or targeting. A web search found no existing Highfleet AI-behaviour mod (nexus mod 63 is a rebalance).
* **Keep the stock tick and override its outputs** (waypoint at `creature+0xbf0/0xbf4` stored at `0x140064a8d`, engine command strength at `0x140065744`, fire bytes, launch gating); do not replace the tick, which also does launches, aircraft, retreat states and ally spacing.
* **Player telemetry is for measuring and tuning hand-built tactics, not for behavioural cloning** (user question, 2026-10-18): the control surface is small (waypoint, thrust, aim, fire, launch), but a few hours from one pilot is too little data for a cloned policy and a state machine can be read and fixed. Tactics (kite, rush, flank, missile boat, break off, overshoot dodge) are code; their numbers (ranges, timings, weights) live in a config file meant to be hot-reloaded. The config is the knobs, not the intelligence.
* **Standalone DLL `hf_ai`** (user chose "B" over putting it in the qol fork), repo `~/Development_Highfleet/hf_ai`, no remote yet. v0.1.0: the steering hook (same trampoline recipe as qol), a CSV of the stock AI's wanted waypoint per ship (`hf_ai_steer_*.csv`, columns include `rel` = angle between the wanted direction and the target bearing), and `decide(&Ctx) -> Option<(f32,f32)>` as the single plug-in point (returns `None`).
* **Planned first tactic:** the overshoot dodge (below).

## Hook ownership (resolved by the port, 2026-10-18: hf_ai v0.2.0 carries `ai_flares`; exactly one of qol `ai_flares.enabled` and hf_ai `ai_flares.enabled` may be on)
`0x140064a8d` is also the site of qol `ai_flares` evasion ([../hooks.md](../hooks.md)); only one mod can own it, because each checks the original bytes before patching. If hf_ai installs first, qol finds a jump there and skips the steering hook: evasion, and also the panic boost and barrel roll (the boost hook is only installed when the steering hook was), go off, while qol's flare hook at `0x14007f864` still installs. Load order is alphabetical in the logs seen so far (`hf_ai` before `highfleet_qol`) [guess, not guaranteed]. **Open decision, not made:** install `hf_ai` now (losing evasion, so the captured enemy behaviour changes) or first build an hf_ai evasion equal to qol's, so nothing is lost (the assistant leans to the second). The hf_ally telemetry already logs `wp_x, wp_y`, so the `hf_ai_steer` CSV adds little until a tactic exists.

## What `ai_flares` does today [code, read in `hf_qol_fork/src/ai_flares.rs`; the numbers are the user's installed `qol.json`, not the code defaults]
Reactive panic against missiles, per AI ship, scanned every 0.1 s:
* Trigger: a missile locked on to the ship (target link `+0x498`, stats category 0x4000 or 0x8000000, active) closer than `trigger_range` 400; roll `reaction_probability` 0.6.
* On success: a flare burst of `burst_seconds` 1.5 s, then `min_interval_seconds` 1.5 s of quiet.
* Evasion (`evade` on): the waypoint is pulled (`evasion_strength` 0.9) towards a sidestep perpendicular to the line to the missile plus 0.5 retreat (`EVASION_RETREAT`), on one random side held for the panic, `evasion_seconds` 3.0; a missile still coming extends it by 1 s (2.5 s for a panic evasion).
* Panic evasion (`panic_evasion_probability` 0.3): a fixed escape direction chosen at the start, engines boosted by `panic_boost` 2.0 for `panic_evasion_seconds` 8.0. Barrel roll (`barrel_roll_probability` 0.01): engine commands rewritten so the ship spins.
* It uses the missile's **position only**: no missile velocity, no time to impact. `allies: true` applies it to AI-run ships of the player's side.

## The overshoot dodge (user's idea, not built)
The user's tactic: do not dodge early; let the missile come, then commit a hard lateral burst late so the missile cannot turn in time and overshoots. For the AI: hold course until time to impact (distance divided by closing speed, from the missile's `+0x130/+0x134` velocity) falls below a threshold, then thrust perpendicular to the missile's **velocity**, and do not spend flares on missiles that will overshoot anyway. Evidence: [ai-telemetry.md](ai-telemetry.md), "Missile overshoot test" (weak). The threshold and the lateral speed needed are to be taken from the v0.12.7 per-frame data.

## Decisions 2026-10-18 (user, after the INTREPID / Lightning / Navarin data) [user]
* **Evasion stays as qol does it.** The user judges the current "break sideways + dump flares" missile evasion already good (it matches an expert's reflex; enemy missiles hit only 2-5% of the time in all 33 sessions). So the overshoot dodge is **not** the first tactic; the first hf_ai version is about **positioning** (fix the stall at 300-500 u inside the player's kill zone, kite band from the ship's own gun reach and acceleration).
* **hf_ai takes over the steering site, so `ai_flares` is ported into hf_ai** (both own `0x140064a8d`/`0x140065744`, only one can). Port first, tune later. Until the port is done, qol `ai_flares.evade` is set to false while hf_ai v0.1.0 (log-only) is installed; enemy flare bursts (separate site) still work.
* **Session 716** (Navarin, vanished at 43% hp) = a retreat. The user chooses between "last stand" and "limp to retreat" once burning and without FSS; enemy AI never gets to retreat.
* **Break-off** (to be defined to the user in plain words): disengaging from a fight you are losing, i.e. turn away, boost out of weapon reach, drop flares, and either leave or stay alive to recover; not decided for AI, and ally behaviour (evade vs last stand) needs more than a state machine: more data (testers) before building.
* **Bombing:** not in hf_ai v1 (the pilot bombs only landed enemies; the AI's target is the airborne player). **Allied bombers** are a requested feature: roadmap item below.

## Prototype spec: "strafe in the band" (hf_ai v0.3.0, enemy positioning; agreed 2026-10-18; BUILT as commit 91e4da2 (local), 47 tests pass, NEVER RUN in game)
Build notes: `tactics_math.rs` (pure, tested), `tactics_config.rs`, `tactics.rs` (game glue); defaults `k_center` 0.32, `band_min` 300, `band_max` 550, `half_width` 100, `outrange_extra` 150, `blend_weight` 1.0, strafe 2-5 s, `min_accel` 35, `retreat_tangent` 0.7, `ground_y` 200 / `ground_clearance` 450 (guess), `edge_margin` 300; arena bounds read live from the four floats at `0x1403c2bc0` (x max, y max, x min, y min); own reach latched (AP drops live reach to 500). **Top risk:** the stock AI often already orbits in this band (steer log: median |rel| 71-75 deg at 100-300 u), so a strafe side opposed to the stock orbit cancels at `blend_weight` 1.0 (the blend falls under 0.2 and the ship stays stock); `blend_weight` 2.0 is the first knob. CSV gained a trailing `state` column (stock/far/band/close/panic).
Goal (user): "give enemy ships some juice". Data basis ([ai-intrepid-benchmark.md](ai-intrepid-benchmark.md)): enemies charge at +45-60 u/s from beyond ~700 u, then stall or back off inside ~400 u and hover at 300-500 u in the player's kill zone; they do not spin; the waypoint is always exactly 200 u from the ship (hf_ai_steer log, 626 rows, 2026-10-18). Built on top of the `ai_flares` port (panic overrides tactics); implementation waits for that commit.
* **Gates (else `decide()` returns `None`, stock behaviour):** AI-flown (`+0xbd4 != 0`), enemy side (`tactics.apply_to_allies` default false), airborne combat mode only (mode at creature `+0xb48`, not the role field `+0xd70`: 1 airborne combat, 3 landed, 0 takeoff; landed and takeoff stay stock), and `accel_est = (+0x1664 + +0x1668) / +0x15b8` above ~35 (Gryphon, Borey, Kadesh, Kastor, Kormoran can't kite and stay stock; see [ship-profiles.md](ship-profiles.md)).
* **Blend, don't replace:** return `ship + 200 * normalize(stock_dir + w * tactic_dir)` (`w` in config), length exactly 200 like stock (unknown whether the length feeds thrust). The stock direction already contains separation/cohesion.
* **Three states by range to the target, band from live gun reach (`+0x1838`):** far = stock (the charge is fine); in band = tangential strafe whose direction reverses on a random 2-5 s timer (fixes the hover); too close = back out on a diagonal, not straight. If the target's reach is longer than ours, hold farther out.
* **Per-ship state** keyed by creature pointer, reset on battle change (pointer reuse bug seen in telemetry).
* **Safety:** no downward component near the ground (in side view "tangential" can be vertical), do not push outward beyond the arena extents (PreTask has an out-of-bounds kill loop), do not fight the stock separation.
* **Config:** `tactics` block in `hf_ai.json` beside `ai_flares`, `enabled` toggle for A/B, optional config hot reload on mtime. **Logging:** fill `chosen_x/chosen_y` and add a `state` column to `hf_ai_steer_*.csv`.
* **Install checklist:** qol `ai_flares.enabled=false` and hf_ai `ai_flares.enabled=true` in the same step (qol still holds the engine-command and flare sites; whichever mod loads second silently loses them).
* **First in-game check (blocks tuning):** `decide()` returning a waypoint has never run; fly one fight and confirm the ship's velocity follows `chosen` within ~1 s.
* **Success metrics (tactics on vs off, same Shipworks setup):** player shell hit proxy in 200-700 u drops, player hp lost per minute rises, time-to-kill rises, no ships hit terrain, leave the arena or collide.

## Decisions 2026-10-18 evening: dive-denial, wrong-foot, curtain, boost (hf_ai v0.4.0 to v0.5.3)
* **Deny the dive was built** as v0.4.0 (`d938255`) from the [ai-underside.md](ai-underside.md) proposal: sidestep when the target hangs within 45 deg of straight under/over, within 600 u, for 1 s; 80% roll once per alignment; own switches `dive_enabled` / `strafe_enabled`; strafe is OFF in the live config because it never showed [data].
* **Verdict v0.4.1** (`tuning_telemetry/dive_v041_report.md`; 4 Intrepid + 4 Lightning sessions, n tiny): the dive fires as designed (66 runs vs 9 skips, 88%) but the effect on the player is small. Under+over share of time within 600 u: Intrepid 56.4% vs 59.3% before, Lightning 54.3% vs 57.9% [data]. Why [data]: the enemy sidesteps about 120 u in 2 s but about 200 u are needed to leave a 45 deg cone, while the player closes about 110 u in the same 2 s. Dive boost 2.0 was added in v0.4.1 (`8ee7aac`); whether enemies really boost is not observable (the telemetry boost column is empty for enemies) [guess: dive speeds look boosted].
* **Wrong-foot** (v0.5.1): choose the dodge side AGAINST the target's sideways motion (above `dive_wrongfoot_speed` 40 u/s), so the player must reverse his momentum; the old coin flip sent the enemy "away" in only 23 of 46 cases [data]. Not yet measured in play.
* **Flare curtain** (v0.5.0 to v0.5.3): decided with the user as a pre-emptive defence, see [ai-flares.md](ai-flares.md#flare-curtain-hf_ai-v050-to-v053-2026-10-18). User feedback: battles feel "a lot more intense", "curtaining looks great", enemies seem to try to get away and keep their distance, top attacks are common [user].
* **Missile dump delay** 0.6-1.5 s (qol `95e8bc7`): early missile deaths near the own hull fell from 19% (21 of 110) to 5% (2 of 45) [data; home-made definition: dies within 2 s near its own hull; Fisher p=0.02].
* **Open design ideas, not built** [user]: armored-sides ships (Intrepid-like) hold height and range; react earlier to fast ships (scale dwell/range with the target's speed); a "dive + overpass" idea whose meaning must be asked of the user first.
