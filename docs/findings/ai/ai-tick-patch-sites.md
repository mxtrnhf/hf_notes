# Tactical AI: close-range cutoff, stand-off, ammo choice (1.163) and their patch sites

Status: **read from the decompilation. In use: the steering site `0x140064a8d`, the engine-command site `0x140065744` and the fire bytes (`hf_ai`, see [../../mods/hf-ai.md](../../mods/hf-ai.md)), and the section 4 launch site, which has a call-site wrapper tested in game (the QoL missile dump: [combat-missile-launch.md](../combat-missile-launch.md)). The other patches are not applied.** Tags: legend in `../../../README.md`.
Addresses are 1.163 (image base `0x140000000`, the unpacked exe). Origin: roadmap tactical AI items ("closing to very short range disables all weapons", "AI consistently gives way to the player", "AP only at extremely short range / LG and INC not used").

## Where the AI lives
* **`FUN_140062f90`** (`0x140062f90`-`0x140065f99`) is the **per-ship AI tick**: steering, launching aircraft and missiles, and the call to the weapon controller. Its one caller is `0x1400739c0`, which Ghidra mislabels `shipKill?`.
* **`FUN_14007a8d0`** (`0x14007a8d0`-`0x14007bfb1`) is the **AI weapon controller**: aims every gun on an AI ship, picks the ammo, decides to fire. 1.151 twin: `0x140076300` (mislabelled `...::atan2f`). Called by the tick when the ship has a target.
* **`creatureLogic`** (`0x140033b90`) is *not* the ship AI: it is the per-body behaviour dispatcher for projectiles, missiles and aircraft, keyed by the `logic` id (`BulletImpact__` stores `ammo->caliber` there). It was never decompiled in one piece [not read].
* Side convention [guess, from where the difficulty byte applies]: the float at `Creature +0xad4`, negative and non-zero = AI side, positive = player's, 0 = neutral. `FUN_140220170(a, b)` is just `a == b` on the int-truncated sides ("same side").

## 1. The close-range cutoff (all guns fall silent when ships touch) [code]
In `FUN_14007a8d0`, after the target's position is read, the function returns before any aiming or `FireGuns` when
`radius(target) + radius(self) + 30.0 > distance`. 1.151's version has no such test (callers not compared), so this is, as far as this function goes, a 1.163 addition.

| Address | Bytes | Instruction |
|---|---|---|
| `0x14007b011` | `f3 0f 10 83 50 18 00 00` | `MOVSS XMM0,[RBX+0x1850]` (target radius) |
| `0x14007b019` | `f3 0f 58 87 50 18 00 00` | `ADDSS XMM0,[RDI+0x1850]` (own radius) |
| `0x14007b021` | `f3 0f 58 05 b3 74 2d 00` | `ADDSS XMM0,[rip+0x2d74b3]` -> the float `30.0` at `0x1403524dc` |
| `0x14007b029` | `41 0f 2f c6` | `COMISS XMM0,XMM14` (XMM14 = centre distance) |
| `0x14007b02d` | `0f 87 0c 0f 00 00` | `JA 0x14007bf3f` (the function's return) |

* `Creature +0x1850` is the ship's **bounding radius** [code]: its only writer is `CalculateStats` at `0x14007ffaa`, from `0x140071120`, the maximum over the root parts of (distance from the centre `+0x1840/+0x1844` + the part's own radius, `Body +0x20c`).
* **Do not patch the constant at `0x1403524dc`**: a pooled `30.0f` with about 60 other users (the `hooks.md` rule).
* **Make the margin adjustable:** operand repoint, change the disp32 at `0x14007b025` (bytes `b3 74 2d 00`) so the `ADDSS` reads a private float in the mod's memory (allocate within +-2 GB of the image). `30.0` is vanilla.
* **Disable the cutoff:** set that float to a large negative value (for example `-1e9`); the sum then never exceeds the distance. (Alternative: overwrite the six `JA` bytes with the 6-byte nop `66 0f 1f 44 00 00`; the float route keeps the knob adjustable.)
* Version guard: compare the 40 bytes from `0x14007b011` to `0x14007b039`. Not owned by any mod in `hooks.md`. Effect untested; the margin only matters when two ships nearly touch.

## 2. Stand-off distance and the "AI gives way" mechanism [code; effect untested]
The steering sums weighted unit vectors. The stand-off is `0.35 x` the larger of two weapon-reach floats (`Creature +0x1838`, `+0x183c`), capped at 1000 (660 if the flag at `+0xc74` is set). The radial weight is `clamp((distance - standoff)/600, -1, 1)` toward the target, so inside the stand-off the weight turns negative and the AI **backs away**, fully retreating 600 units inside it
(a 2000-reach ship starts backing off at 700). A perpendicular **orbit** term with weight `1 - |radial|` makes it circle at the stand-off. Other terms: separation from **allies** (same int side; it does *not* push away from the player unless two mode flags are set, `DAT_147fc6fe0` and a reach >= 6000 condition), cohesion toward the group, a waypoint pull, map-edge push.
This retreat, not separation, is the likely reason the AI "consistently gives way to the player": nothing steers the player. **[guess]**

| Knob | Instruction (address, bytes) | Constant | Other users of the constant |
|---|---|---|---|
| stand-off = `0.35` x reach | `0x1400631a6` `f3 0f 59 1d 0e 15 2f 00` and `0x1400631ec` `f3 0f 59 1d c8 14 2f 00` (`MULSS XMM3,[rip+d]`; with / without a ship target) | `0.35f` at `0x1403546bc` | 6 (`SomethingWithLevelSerias` x4, `FUN_14012f120`, one more) |
| stand-off cap 1000 | `0x14006320d` `f3 0f 10 35 2f f6 2e 00` (`MOVSS XMM6,[rip+d]`, then `MINSS XMM3,XMM6` at `0x14006321e`) | `1000.0f` at `0x140352844` | many (also the AP range threshold below) |
| stand-off cap 660 (flag `+0xc74`) | `0x1400631fe` `f3 0f 5d 1d ce 33 2f 00` (`MINSS XMM3,[rip+d]`) | `660.0f` at `0x1403565d4` | not checked |
| radial width 600 | `0x140064723` `f3 0f 5e 05 11 e1 2e 00` (`DIVSS XMM0,[rip+d]`; result clamped to +-1) | `600.0f` at `0x14035283c` | about 24 |

Recipes (operand repoint to a private float, with a version guard around each site): a smaller `0.35` makes the AI hold a shorter range (it closes in); a larger `600` flattens the retreat; a very large `600` means it almost never backs away. Orbit and separation are unchanged by these.

## 3. AI ammo choice (AP only close; never LG or INC) [code, same rule in 1.151]
For guns whose part caliber index is 15, 20, 25 or 30 (100, 130, 180, 203mm) the controller sets the gun's ammo slot (1.163 gun `+0x850`) with `FindAmmoIndex(name)`: **`_AP` only if two creature flags (`+0xc7c`/`+0xc74`) are set and the target is closer than 1000** (`0x14007b449`: `COMISS XMM6,XMM14` with the pooled `1000.0f`); otherwise the proximity variant under other flags (`+0xc7d`, `+0xc75`/`+0xc76`); otherwise the plain shell. It **never selects `_INC` or `_LG`**, the cause of "the AI cannot use LG/INC shells".
* **Checked 2026-10-20 (four DeepSeek readings, the AP gate and the branch condition re-read by Claude) [agent, code where stated]:** the assignment runs once per controller call for gun **group 0 only** (`creature +0x13a8..+0x13b0`), not group 1; it runs when the ship target `creature +0xb00` is set and the distance is at least `radius(target)+radius(self)+30.0` [code]. With only a missile/aircraft target (`+0xb18` set, `+0xb00` null) the function aims, fires and returns before the ammo block [code]. The distance is a real (square-rooted) distance [agent]. `+0xc74/+0xc75/+0xc76` are re-rolled every 30 s (`+0xc78`) by `AiRerollAmmoFlags` `0x140079810` from the target's stats (`+0xc75`: `rand < (T+0x1604 * 0.125)^2`; `+0xc74`: `rand < (2 * T+0x15bc / T+0x15b8)^2`; `+0xc76`: `rand < armour table T+0x1568[octant] * 0.5 (or 0.25)`) and only if the target's class code & `0x2f` == `0x2f` [agent]. `+0xc74` also caps the stand-off at 660 [agent]. No `_INC`/`_LG` name is referenced by the controller [agent]; `loadPrecombatAmmo?` (`0x140260680`, can write `_AP`/`_PROXY`/`_INC` by preset) is called only from `precombatOk` and only works on the player's squad list `0x147fc73b0` [code, Claude].
* **[game, user 2026-10-20] The AI does use AP and proximity shells; AP, proximity and HE are the only shell types it uses.** That fits the controller (it is the only routine that picks a ship gun's ammo: the `FindAmmoIndex` callers are this controller, the loadouts and shops, and UI/reticle code). **OPEN: who sets `+0xc7c` / `+0xc7d` on an enemy ship.** Facts: the Creature constructor zeroes them (`MOV word [RDI+0xc7c],BP` at `0x1400730e7`; `EBP` is set once by `XOR EBP,EBP` at `0x140072bc2` and never rewritten before it) [code, Claude]; no hit for these offsets in any data section (byte search `7C 0C 00 00` / `7D 0C 00 00`: all hits are in code) [code, Claude]. Stores that DO write `+0xc7c`: `0x1400c7f3c..0x1400c7f60` (`if (c7c == 0) { +0x8f8 *= 0.5; c7c = 1 }`, inside the function at `0x1400c7400`, whose only call is from `FUN_1400beea0` at `0x1400bf2f4`) and `0x1400d4549..0x1400d4566` (the reverse: `if (c7c != 0) { +0x8f8 *= 2; c7c = 0 }`, over a list of objects) [code, Claude]. What object these write (a Creature or a GUI/editor object) is **not established**; the agents called them GUI/editor classes without proof, and an earlier guess here (Shipworks editor plus the field copy at `0x14012c504`) is dropped. **Update 2026-10-20 (later):** the first store site writes a fresh `New(0xe70)` object built by the GUI constructor `0x14012a910` (the second loops over a list of such objects), so both stores are GUI-element state, not ships [code, Claude]. So no code store sets these bytes on a Creature: the logging build `hf_ally_v0.12.11-ammo.1` (file `hf_ally_ammo_*.csv`, installed) answers it live. Existing sim logs [game]: in session 1792456093 one enemy Archangel starts with `ITEM_AMMO_180MM_PROXY` guns (others plain), so the proximity flag does get set on some enemy ships; Wasp (session 1792451176) shows `ITEM_UNDEF` (ammo index 0) on all 6 guns; cause unknown. It is not the `ITEM_AMMO_230MM` lookup miss: `OL.seria` has no part with `m_weapon_caliber=30` (0 exact matching lines, checked 2026-10-20), so the old note stands [file]. Probably these are guns of another kind (their stats `+0x144` is not one of 15/20/25/30) whose ammo slot was never set [guess].
* **Latent bug in both versions:** for caliber index 30 it looks up `ITEM_AMMO_230MM` (and `_PROXY`, `_AP`) but the table has `ITEM_AMMO_203MM`; `FindAmmoIndex` returns 0 on a miss (the undefined entry, a `FireShell` default: muzzle flash only). Harmless in vanilla: no `OL.seria` part has `m_weapon_caliber=30` [game]; the lookup miss was confirmed by four readers [agent]. (A DeepSeek claim that 'every 203 mm AI gun loads the blank shell' is moot for the same reason: there are none.)
* [game] `m_weapon_caliber` equals the ammo `index`: `MDL_CANNON_30_6` 5, `MDL_CANNON_57_2` 10, `MDL_CANNON_100_2` 15, `MDL_CANNON_130` / `_LOW` 20, `MDL_CANNON_180` / `_180_2` 25, **`MDL_CANNON_305_2` also 25** (180mm shells), `MDL_RSZO_220` 52, `MDL_TORPEDO_300` 54, `MDL_CANNON_HARPOON` 90, `MDL_KAZ` 100.

## 4. Launching from the AI tick [code]
The tick launches with `LaunchZenithAtOwnerTarget` (`FUN_14002ff60`) (call at `0x140065edb`) when the target is between `radius(target) + radius(self) + 100` and 2000 away, on a roughly 1% random roll per tick, with a 2.0 s ship cooldown (`+0xd60`) and a 6.0 s timer (`+0xcb8`), over the launch list `+0x13d8`. **This is the Zenith (anti-ship missile) launch** [game, see [combat-missile-launch.md](../combat-missile-launch.md)]; earlier text called it the aircraft launch, and whether aircraft use it is not established.
The AI tick also has call sites for `FUN_14002e3a0` (`0x140065880`, `0x140065f7f`; the multicharge combat launch, see `hooks.md`) over the ship's second list (`+0x13f0`). These are the **anti-air launchers, not Zenith**: hooked, never reached in three battles [game]; the rest of this paragraph is [code, not observed in play]. Per-ship caps against special targets: **3 launches vs a strategic launch, 1 vs an aircraft** (counter `+0xc98`, 0.5 s apart at `+0xd64`). **Counter details, checked 2026-10-20 [code, disassembly `0x140065830..0x14006589f`]:** `CMOVNZ EBX,3` after the `0x8000000` flag test, `CMOVNZ EBX,R13D` (=1) after the `0x400` test, `CMP [R14+0xc98],EBX; JGE skip`; the loop over `+0x13f0..+0x13f8` calls `FUN_14002e3a0` at `0x140065880`; `INC [R14+0xc98]` only on success; then `+0xd64 = 0.5f`. The counter is zeroed only when the creature is created (`0x1402d4bf0` in `FUN_1402d45c0`) and no other zeroing store was found [agent: literal-offset search], so it is a whole-battle budget per ship, not per target. The launcher lists are built in the per-part stats pass (`FUN_140071380`, called from `CalculateStats` at `0x140080212`, parts with class code `[part+0x28] == 0xf`) split by the part's logic id `+0x3c4`: ids 1 and 132 go to `+0x13d8` (Zenith), id 3 to `+0x13f0` (anti-air), both only while the part's `+0x848` is 0.0 [agent].
`FUN_14002e3a0` adds, for **AI-side launchers only** (owner side negative and non-zero): at most **1 launch per second at the same target** (`target +0x8b0` holds the last time; its only writers in the exe are the stores at `0x14002e557` and `0x14002e797`), at most **10** missiles in flight at one target, **2 at a tactical missile (`0x4000`), 1 at an aircraft (`0x400`)**, minus those already tracking it (compared at `+0x498`); and the launcher timer `+0x3c0` is `FUN_1401af7b0(8)`, or `(6)` when `Profile +0x164` is set. Player-side launchers skip all of this. None of these limits applies to Zenith.

Four f32 at `0x1403c2bc0`: x max, y max, x min, y min. `AiShipTick` reads them at `0x140064673`; `SomethingWithLevelSerias` writes +-6000 at `0x1402c36b6`; the deploy code uses the same addresses. ## 5. When AI main guns fire (Ghidra read, 2026-10-18 evening; subagent, read-only) [code unless tagged]
* `FireGuns` (`0x1400559e0`) takes ONE gun Body (RCX), not the creature; callers loop the gun lists themselves: group 0 = creature `+0x13a8..+0x13b0`, group 1 = `+0x13c0..+0x13c8`, stride 0x18, gun at `+8`. It checks gun `+0x2f4 == 0`, `+0x818 != 0`, stats `+0x820`, then `FireGun` (`0x140032e60`): fires only if gun `+0x860` (loaded) >= 1 and `+0x870 <= 0` and `+0x86c` (rate timer) == 0, then `+0x860 -= 1`, `+0x86c = 60/rpm`. A second call in the same frame returns early (no double fire).
* `AiWeaponController` (`0x14007a8d0`) aims ALL guns of both groups at one angle before any range/angle/ammo test (guns track continuously, only the fire call is gated). Per-gun order: latch gun `+0x875 != 0` jumps straight to FireGuns (skips range, angle, ally tests until the magazine empties); `+0x860 <= 0` skip; `creature +0x228` set and dist > 700 skip; zoom-level condition and caliber <= 10 and dist > 1500 skip; **range test** `0x14007ba84` (group 0) / `0x14007bd55` (group 1): `COMISS XMM8,XMM1; JA skip` with range = creature `+0x1838` (group 0) / `+0x183c` (group 1) x 2.0 only if the camera zoom level >= 4 or the ship is outside the level bounds, else x 1.0 (dist = root body to the predicted aim point); **angle tolerance** |gun `+0x138` - aim angle| <= 0.025 rad (private constant `0x140354678`); **friendly-fire** check (segment 2000 long, 3.0 radii) when the player-side list has > 1 entry; **start condition** magazine FULL (`+0x860 >= stats +0x148`) sets latch `+0x875`. No timer, no random roll in the controller.
* `creature +0xbbc/+0xbbd` (group 0/1 fire bytes, the player's LMB/RMB) are never touched by the AI controller; `CreatureUpdate` consumes them (`0x14007f799` / `0x14007f7d9`: FireGuns for every gun of the group, each gun obeying its own reload). Writers: constructor, player input (`FUN_14018af40`, `FUN_1401896a0`, `FUN_14018a530`), zeroing sites `0x14020f226`, `0x140232784`, `0x14024c0d9`. A value a mod writes on an AI ship persists until cleared and fires the group [data, CONFIRMED at runtime 2026-10-18 with hf_ai v0.6.4: enemy `f0 = 1` in 3% of rows, 34 episodes, 13 of them produced shells]. The friendly-fire list `0x147fc73d8` being the player-side set is a [guess]. hf_ai v0.6.1 early fire uses this (option A). Alternatives not used: latch `+0x875 = 1` (vanilla burst but only while the controller runs), a stub at the two range-test sites (widen range for chosen ships; angle/ally tests still apply). Do NOT scale `+0x1838/+0x183c` (stand-off distance uses them) or the shared constants `0x140352840`, `0x1403548b0`.

## Annotated sites (Ghidra pass of 2026-10-18; the comments are saved in the program)
* `0x14007ba84`: AI RANGE GATE group 0: `COMISS XMM8,XMM1 ; JA skip`. XMM1 = creature `+0x1838` (x2.0 if zoom >= 4 or outside the level bounds, else x1.0).
* `0x14007bd55`: AI RANGE GATE group 1 (same test, creature `+0x183c`).
* `0x14007f799`: `CMP byte [creature+0xbbc],0`: the player's LMB (group-0 fire byte); set means `FireGuns` for every gun of group 0. The AI controller never sets it; `hf_ai` early fire writes it. The `hf_ai` flare hook is at `0x14007f864` in the same function.
* `0x14007f7d9`: `CMP byte [creature+0xbbd],0`: the player's RMB (group-1 fire byte), same handling.
* `0x140065edb`: `CALL LaunchZenithAtOwnerTarget` (enemy Zenith launch over the launch list `+0x13d8`); the QoL missile dump wraps this call (the roll is at `0x140065c6c`, the launch here).
* `0x14018a7c9`: `CALL LaunchZenithAtOwnerTarget`: the PLAYER launch path uses it too (player missiles carry target link `+0x490/+0x498` and owner link `+0x4a8/+0x4b0`).
* `0x140354678`: `0.025` rad: the AI gun angle tolerance (`|gun +0x138 - aim angle|`), a PRIVATE constant (single xref `0x14007b959`).
