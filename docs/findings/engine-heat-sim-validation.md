# Engine heat in the simulator: what was checked against the logs (simulator session, 2026-10-21/22)

Tags: **[log]** measured on sim.35 to sim.37 hook rows (EA/EG/EK/EZ/EL, XU), sessions 1792612319, 1792613461 (Kormoran, Shipworks); **[sim]** result of replaying the logged keys through `hf_ml/sim/hfsim/flight.py`. The game rule itself is in [player-thrust-keys-and-engine-heat.md](player-thrust-keys-and-engine-heat.md) sections 5 to 8.

* **Dose per call [log]:** the logged force fraction (EK, = g x strength) equals the sim's to 0.1 to 1.6 %; at strength 1 the observed heating at T < 40 is 1.25 per tick against 1.40 predicted (`(g*s)^2 * 70 * dt`), the rest is conduction.
* **Conduction [log + code]:** the measured extra heat loss of a hot engine grows with T (about 0.005 per tick per degree) and is the 0.008 x (T - Tn) per cooler slot neighbour of Body_ThermalStep. Every Kormoran engine has exactly ONE slot neighbour in the design graph (a MDL_FERMA4X4_01 truss for the two RD-59 swivels, MDL_FERMA2X2_01/02 for the D-30S statics); the graph is the `Slot` records (`m_master.id`, `m_block.id`) of the design file.
* **Sim rule [sim]:** all body parts take part (decay, downhill flow to strictly cooler slot neighbours in turn, engines above 400 gain 0.8 per tick), no fitted constant. First trips: 1792612319 all 12 trips within 0.06 s; 1792613461 first burst within 0.08 s, later trips up to 0.4 s late (the sim engines run 3 to 14 degrees cool at the trip).
* **Extinguisher (key B) [log]:** every player engine (cold ones too) goes to T 100 (100 to 105 a tick later), lock 0, dose 0. Each press uses one module; Kormoran has four, the second ship two. A used module is removed from the creature's list, not set to charge 0 (probe-dev, sim.38), so there is no reload. The 30-unit radius could not be checked (XN rows read the wrong list before sim.38).
* **Open [log]:** after an extinguisher use the swivel engines heat 25 to 40 degrees slower in the sim than in the game (log 480, sim 438 at 12.5 s of 1792613461). The measured heat loss after the reset is half of the first heat-up's (0.56 against 1.09 per tick at T 100 to 200), so the truss must be about 80 degrees in the game against 54 in the sim. Parts without burn stats are not in the T rows, so this could not be checked; sim.38 TT/TA rows log the neighbours and every part.
* **No global constant fixes it [sim]:** sweeping the conduction divisor, the dose, the burn heat and the onward-conduction scale improved 1792613461 only by hurting 1792612319 (error 0.037 s to 0.29 s), so the rule was left as decoded.
* **Spin of the Kormoran [sim]:** the logged top spin is 0.54 rad/s; the sim with the lockout gives 0.52, without it 0.79.
* **Tooling:** `hf_ml/sim/simdiff <file.hftm>` replays a battle, re-seeds the sim from the log every 3 s, names the first channel that leaves tolerance and re-runs the window with the logged force / apply mask / engine temperature to say which part of the model is at fault. Checked on injected faults (no burn heat: sim late by 26 to 32 degrees; dose +21 %: sim early by 10 to 87 degrees, both reported as engine heat).
