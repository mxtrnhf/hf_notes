# Shell blast, armour shadows, penetration chain and flare rule (1.163)

Status: read in Ghidra 2026-10-21 [code]; roles approximate where marked. Related: [explosion-damage-1151-vs-1163.md](explosion-damage-1151-vs-1163.md), [contact-damage-and-fire.md](contact-damage-and-fire.md), [guns-aim-reload-fire.md](guns-aim-reload-fire.md).

Read today in 1.163 ([code]); details of the occlusion model NOT decoded.

* **Detonation trigger** (see also guns-aim-reload-fire.md section 7: a plain shell has 1 hp and dies from contact damage, whose death method is the blast; the impulse path below applies to projectiles with `0 <= +0x38c < I`; the probe shows plain shells do take it, see shell-detonation-paths.md): `Projectile_DetonateOnImpulse 0x14001d1e0` calls the shell's vtable `+0x200` (the blast routine, unnamed `FUN_140029b60`) when the shell has explosive `+0x2dc != 0` and contact impulse > `shell+0x38c` (>= 0). Shells also expire by ttl. 
* **Blast routine `FUN_140029b60`** (runs once per detonation): E = shell `+0x2dc`; scale `S = 2*sqrt(E)`, radius `R = sqrt(E)`; `S` multiplied by **0.66 unless the shooter is the player ship** (player 1.0; the 0.66 also applies to the player's shell when `BOOL_147fc6f80/DAT_147fc6f81` and `Profile+0x165` are set). Blast centre = the shell position, or for a shell that is attached to a body (`+0x1fc`/link `+0x3b0`) the hit point found by stepping along the shell's velocity direction (up to 3 steps of `+0x20c` radius) against the hit body. Candidates: every Body in `somethingBodyArray_` (15000 slots) that is alive, class low nibble 0xf, not the shell, not the hit body, flag word `+0x1c0 & 0x405 != 0x405`; frames (class 0x1f) are expanded to their child parts; each candidate needs distance(centre, part centre) - part radius `+0x20c` < R (R raised to 200 when the shell has penetration `+0x2e0 != 0` and R <= 200).
* **Per-part damage** `Body_AreaDamageFromBlast 0x14002cdc0(part, shell, scale, R, centre, &out, incendiary, flag)`: `d = floor(5 * scale * (1 - dist_edge/R)^2)`; x5 scale if the part has aircraft logic (`+0xa20`); **armour parts (Stats cat bit 0x2) hit by a plain shell (flags `&0x405 == 0x405`) with explosive <= 100 take d/5**; then `Body_ApplyDamage` (armour threshold from Stats `+0xc4`). Output fraction = clamp(part hp / d, 0, 1), used by the caller as shielding. Incendiary: dose `+0x2f0 += blastTerm * incendiary(+0x2e8) * (0.5 for the player ship)`.
* **Direct hit** on the body the shell touched (`shell+0x3b0`): plain explosive shell: blast damage with `scale * fraction`; the penetrating (AP) branch (`Stats cat & 2` armour target) uses the same call then a directional occlusion table of 360 one-degree buckets (`FUN_14002d610`, `FUN_140045800`) so parts BEHIND the hit part along the shell's direction are shadowed by its hp.
* **Penetration chain**: `strength = min(1, shell +0x2c0-ish factor + 0.5) * penetration(+0x2e0)`; each part along the path that has hp is damaged by `strength*4` (direct) and reduces strength by `part hp/20` (parts of Stats category 0x80, the FERMA / HARD truss and hardpoint parts, do not reduce it; corrected 2026-10-21, see flares-and-missiles.md) and by 10 per pierced part. AP shells have explosive 1.0 so R = 1, i.e. all their damage comes from this chain, not from the area blast.
* **Proximity/truss rule** (`b2CListener_PreSolve 0x140284730`; corrected 2026-10-21: category 0x80 is the FERMA / HARD truss and hardpoint parts, NOT flares): when an explosive projectile (flags `&0xd == 0xd`, explosive > 0) touches a body whose Stats category is exactly 0x80, with probability 0.9 (rand > 0.1) a blast with scale `2*sqrt(E)/10` and radius `sqrt(E)` centred on the decoy fires once per (shell, decoy) pair (list at shell `+0x898`), and the contact is disabled (shell passes through). 
* After the blast the routine spawns airblast particles, camera shake (distance-scaled, halved/boosted by `Profile`) and records the blast in `DAT_1439e6038`.
* NOT decoded: exact occlusion buckets; how the shell's `+0x3b0` hit-body link is set (set at contact time); shell gravity/drag (`ap_drag +0x1dc` is an AP-only drag); whether the ship velocity is inherited.

## Occlusion table decoded (2026-10-21, [code] FUN_14002d610 + FUN_140045800 + the loop in FUN_140029b60; roles approximate)
* The "360 buckets" are a local array of 360 floats initialised to 1.0 per blast (a transparency per whole degree around the blast centre). Candidates are processed NEAREST FIRST (the candidate records, 0x28 bytes each with distance, angle range `[a0,a1]` in degrees and an in-path flag, are sorted by distance before the loop).
* `FUN_140045800(table, a0, a1, w)` = sum of `w * table[i]` over the part's angular span with `w = 1/(span+1)`: the mean remaining transparency (visibility) over the span, 0..1. A part's blast damage = `Body_AreaDamageFromBlast(part, shell, visibility * S, R, ...)`.
* `FUN_14002d610(table, a0, a1, amount)` subtracts `amount` from every bucket of the span (wraps around 359->0), clamped at 0. `amount` = the damage routine's output fraction = clamp(part hp / damage dealt, 0, 1) (so a part with hp >= the damage it takes blocks fully, a weaker part blocks hp/damage).
* **Only ARMOUR parts (Stats category bit 0x2) cast shadows**: in the armour branch the call is `AreaDamage(part, ..., visibility * S * k)` with `k = clamp(S/5, 0, 1)` (small shells do little to armour) followed by the shadow subtraction. Non-armour parts take `visibility * S` and do NOT reduce the table. So the order is: the near armour plates shield everything behind them within their angular span; ordinary parts do not shield each other from the area blast.
* The penetration chain is separate: parts flagged "in the shell's path" (centre within the shell radius of the ray, flag at record `+4`) take `strength * 4` direct damage (if alive and `part hp != 0`) and reduce `strength` by `part+0x2cc(max hp)/20`-ish (field `pfVar10[0xb3]`/20 for non-decoy parts) and by 10; this is how AP and big shells go through several parts in a line.
* Result: area damage is a nearest-first shadow cast; to simulate: sort parts by distance, for each compute span from the two tangent directions, visibility from the table, damage, and only if armour subtract `min(1, hp/damage)` from the span.
