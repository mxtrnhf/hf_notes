Status: **read in Ghidra 2026-10-20 [code]**; formulas not yet compared with a fire or ram in the logs. Source report with all addresses: `hf_ml/out/ds2-contact-fire/REPORT.md`. Tags: legend in `../../README.md`.

## Contact (collision) damage
* Handler `FUN_1402849a0(rec)` (callers `+0x2856c0` / `+0x2857ab` of the log): `rec+0x08` / `rec+0x20` = the two bodies, `rec+0x40` = raw impulse. The scale constant `__05` is the float `0.05` at `0x1403c0e10`, so impulse `I = rec[+0x40] / 0.05^4 = rec[+0x40] * 160000`, damage `d = I * 5e-6 = 0.8 * rec[+0x40]` [code]. Masses are not in the damage formula (only in the hit-sound volume); they act inside the physics solver that produced the impulse.
* Each body gets `body.vtable+0x210 (FUN_1400519d0)(body, d, 1, 1, flag, 0)`. **Tear-off:** if the part's master (`+0x88`) is a class-`0x1f` Frame and `I > 4,000,000` (`8,000,000` when `DAT_147fc6fd4`), no damage is dealt; `FUN_140286100(part)` detaches the part into a free body that inherits the Frame's linear and angular velocity [code]. Projectiles: when `0 <= body[+0x38c] < I`, `FUN_14001d1e0` explodes a body that has `+0x2dc` explosive (harpoon, logic `0x87`, sticks and adds 10 to `+0x844` of the target's class-`0xf` parts) [code].
* **The damage slot `FUN_1400519d0`:** return 0 if `d == 0`, part `+0x2d0` set (invulnerable) or `hp (+0x2c8) <= 0`; armour threshold: `d < Stats +0xc4` gives 0 when `p3 != 0` and `p5 == 0`; when `p5 != 0` (hull touches hull) or a leg part in campaign flight (`DAT_147fc6fd4`) the threshold is `ship mass (+0x15b8) / 1e6` (x10 when `BOOL_147fc6f80 || DAT_147fc6f81`). **Player-ship scaling x0.6:** applies to the player's own ship when (`BOOL_147fc6f80 == 0 && DAT_147fc6f81 == 0`) and `Profile +0x165 == 0`, or otherwise when `Profile +0x164 != 0`; explains the 0.6-multiples of the logged player drops. Then `hp = clamp(hp - d, 0, +0x2cc)` through the set-health slot `vtable+0x208` [code].
* **Where `rec` comes from (read 2026-10-20 [code]):** the engine is **Box2D** (strings `Init_Box2D`, RTTI `b2ContactListener` / `b2CListener` / `b2CFilter` / `b2DListener`, joint-def dumps incl. wheel and rope joints, so 2.3 or later; world singleton `SuperBig` `0x143a119f0`, stepped in `SomethingPhysicTask` by `FastFail_(SuperBig, 1/coreFrameRateFlt_, 10)` at `0x1402f0ca0`). The game's listener function (entry before `0x140284440`, undefined in Ghidra; args `RDX` = `b2Contact*`, `R8` = `const b2ContactImpulse*`, i.e. `PostSolve`) reads fixtures at `contact+0x60/+0x68`, the manifold at `contact+0x78` (point count at `contact+0xb4`), calls `b2WorldManifold::Initialize` (`0x140318330`), and allocates a **0x48-byte record** (vtable `0x140352310`; Link to body A at `+8`, to body B at `+0x20`; world contact point at `+0x30/+0x34`, normal at `+0x38/+0x3c`; **`+0x40` = the maximum of `normalImpulses[0..count-1]`** and `+0x44` = the tangent impulse with the largest magnitude) and appends it to the list `DAT_143a33c48`. `SomethingPhysicTask` walks that list after the step, calling `FUN_1402849a0(rec)` once per record, then frees it. A contact that stays touching yields a new record every step. Both bodies get the identical `d = rec[+0x40] * 160000 * 5e-6` (one shared variable); differences between the two sides arise only inside the damage slot, or because one side is skipped. Contacts above the tear-off impulse never reach the slot (no damage line): only `FUN_140286100` runs.
* Open: the physical units of `rec[+0x40]` (Box2D impulse scaled by the game's `0.05` constant); which body types count as "hull" (`vtable+0x1a8`); meaning of the other skip flags.

## Fire (heat) model
* Only parts with burn stats can burn: fuel tanks `MDL_FUEL_01/02/03` (`m_burn_ignite` 200, `m_burn_heat` 200, `m_burn_hp_max` 100 / 800 / 100) and every `MDL_ENGINE_*` (ignite 400, heat 10) [file: `OL.seria`]; Stats `+0xbc` = ignite, `+0xc0` = heat [code + names].
* Body fields: `+0x2ec` temperature, `+0x2f0` incendiary dose, `+0x2f4` eight flame floats (visual), `+0x334` per-part burn value = the Body save key **`m_burn_hp`** (e.g. 40 on `MDL_ENGINE_01` in `parts.seria`; `SerializeHeader` reads `+0x334` at `0x140050589` and writes that key at `0x140050598` [code]); static 0 / 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 100 in 12,705 logged parts, never changes [game], probably because no logged part burned, `+0x220` visited mark, `+0x804` crew in the part.
* **Source:** blast area damage `FUN_14002cdc0` adds `falloff-term * explosion temperature (shell +0x2e8) * (0.5 for the player's ship under the same Profile byte test)` to `+0x2f0` [code].
* **Thermal step** `FUN_140055870(part, dt, heat, ambient, k)` (a Body vtable method): `T += (k * 5.9523813e-5 + 0.008333334) * (ambient - T) * dt + heat * [+0x2f0]`, then conduction to every cooler joined part (slot vector `+0x350`, up to 99): `flow = (T - T_n) / 10 * dt` [code]. **Burn update** `FUN_140054850`: ignites when `T >= Stats +0xbc` ("ON FIRE" for the player's ship; flames random 0.5..1.0, or 1.0..1.5 for a fuel-holding part); while burning: hp damage `min(+0x334, 0.25 * dt)` per call (armour threshold skipped, `p3 = 0`), `T += dt * Stats +0xc0` (a burning tank adds 200 per second, runaway; an engine 10), and 5% chance per call to lose 1 crew from the part when it holds crew [code]. Below ignite the flames go out.
* Open: who calls the thermal step and with what heat / ambient / `k` (vtable slot only); where `+0x2f0` is reset; units of `dt`; whether `+0x334` (`m_burn_hp`) is a pool that drops while burning (no decrement seen in the burn update).
