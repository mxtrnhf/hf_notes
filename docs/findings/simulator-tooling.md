# Simulator tooling and validation status (simulator session, 2026-10-22)

Code: `hf_ml/sim` (local git, no remote). Data: `hf_ml/data/sim_runs28..30` (.hftm files).

* **Ship from the dump:** `hfsim/dumpship.py` rebuilds the flown ship from `hf_ally_ship_<id>_<n>.seria` (root Frame, children relative to it; the Frame's own `m_position`/`m_angle` come AFTER its children), `_extra.seria` (parentless jointed parts, world coordinates) and the sim.39 simparts `J` rows (`j_a_id`/`j_b_id`/`j_master_id` = m_id of the end Bodies, shifts, angle limits, motor, length; kind 0 distance, 1 revolute, 2 weld). The Frame is moved to the origin, extras are moved into its axes, each J row becomes a Joint node. Two fixes were needed: landing legs are dumped in their standing pose while the design convention (and the sim's folding) starts from joint angle 0 (body angle = reference when the joint's A is a Frame-tree part, A's angle + reference when A is a leg; shiftA is in A's own axes), and ANTENNA_PART markers (more in the dump than in designs) carry no physics and their weld joints change the inertia, so they are dropped. Result for Kormoran, Fenek, Intrepid: exact oid list, same Frame inertia and centre of mass as the library design to four digits.
* **Dump facts:** the Kormoran has 50 J rows against 42 design joints (the 8 extra are weld joints between antenna markers); Frame `+0x218/+0x21c` logged by sim.39 equal the Frame_RecomputeBounds port (92.79 x 61.44, 49.72 x 31.95, 35.47 x 31.53); the centre `+0x210/+0x214` is relative to the Frame origin.
* **Design variants:** ships with the same part list but different engine bank codes exist (Lightning, Lightning_Stock, Lightning_save): the part list alone cannot choose; a wrong variant fires the wrong static engine under Q/E and looks like a high-spin physics error. Without a dump, `simdiff` picks the variant whose banks agree with the logged commands (steps where two command angles occur).
* **Key timing:** the P rows (keys) are 30 Hz, so a key edge is known to +-33 ms, enough to move a Lightning's velocity by about 2 u/s. Strength and fire state are taken from the EA rows (50 Hz) in open-loop replays; whole commands only in re-seeded windows. The logged command ANGLE must not be used in long open-loop runs (it belongs to the logged hull angle).
* **Truss temperatures (sim.38 TT rows):** every player engine has exactly one slot neighbour (a truss); the sim's onward conduction from non-engine parts is too strong by about 20 %, fitted scale 0.8 over four sessions (probable cause: the game's slot lists give the truss fewer cooler neighbours than the design graph).
* **Stabiliser:** all engine calls of a tick read the same stale w and angle (ghidra-curator); the gate counts only existing engines, a locked-out engine still counts (curator verified); no player engine ever has +0x848 != 0 in 160k logged calls.
* **Tool `simdiff <hftm>`:** open-loop replay, then re-seeds the sim every 3 s from the log, names the first channel that leaves tolerance (swivel angle, temperature, lock, force, w, angle, velocity, position) and re-runs each flagged window with the logged force fractions, apply mask or engine temperature to say which part of the model is at fault. Tested on injected faults. Windows within 120 u of the ground are ignored (scope: mid-air combat only).
* **Open:** Intrepid (88 degrees drift, 1792619889), Fenek (fast spin 4 to 7 rad/s with two engines, 1792620006), Kormoran heat trips 3 to 14 degrees late, Lightning A/D windows at 200 to 450 u/s. Open-loop end errors over 75 to 110 s: Kormoran 38 to 351 u, Lightning 380 to 400 u, Gladiator 109 u, Wanderer 53 u.
